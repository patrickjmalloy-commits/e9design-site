Services

One team for the whole room.

A golf room is more than a simulator. We design the space, build it, and install the technology, so everything works together and looks like it belongs.

Residential

Home golf rooms

Basements, garages and bonus rooms, turned into a place to practice, play and entertain.

  • Dedicated rooms finished like the rest of your home
  • Garage builds that still work as a garage
  • Seating, bar areas, lighting and sound

Commercial

Commercial golf spaces

Golf as a feature of your business: client entertainment, team events, or a draw for your guests.

  • Offices, bars, restaurants, clubs and event venues
  • Rooms that also work for meetings and presentations
  • Durable materials for heavy use

Simulation

Overhead, out of the way.

Most of our builds use the ProTee VX, an overhead launch monitor mounted on the ceiling above the hitting area. Nothing sits on the floor to trip over or hit, and the room stays clean for right- and left-handed golfers.

What goes into a golf room

Every part, planned together.

Space planning

Swing clearance, sightlines, seating and traffic flow, worked out before anything is built.

Simulation

Overhead ProTee VX launch monitor, computer and course software, set up and tested.

Screen & enclosure

A quiet, durable impact screen with padded surrounds to keep the room safe.

Projection

A projector sized and placed for a bright picture, with no shadow on the screen.

Turf & hitting area

A mat that’s easy on your joints, set into turf that finishes the room.

Lighting, sound & finishes

Lighting, audio, wall treatments, storage and furniture, so it feels like a finished room.
