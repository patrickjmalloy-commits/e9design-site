Why “Emergency 9”

For the round you thought you didn’t have time for.

In golf, an “emergency nine” is the quick round you squeeze in when eighteen holes won’t fit the day. That’s the idea behind everything we build: real golf, at home or at work, whenever you have an hour.

  • No tee times, no weather, no drive to the course
  • Practice with real swing data, or play famous courses with friends
  • A room you’ll actually use, designed around your space and budget

Questions

Common questions

How much space do I need for a golf simulator?

A common starting point is a ceiling of about 9–10 feet, a width of 12–16 feet and a depth of 15–20 feet, but every room is different. Ceiling height matters most for a full driver swing. We measure your space and tell you honestly what will work before you spend anything.

Can I put a simulator in my garage and still park there?

Often, yes. A retractable screen and a roll-up or removable hitting area can let a garage work as a golf bay at night and a garage during the day. We'll check ceiling height, door tracks and lighting on the site visit.

What does a golf simulator cost?

It depends mostly on the launch monitor and how finished you want the room. We don't push the most expensive setup. After a showroom visit and a look at your space, you get a written quote with options at different budgets.

Can I try one before I buy?

Yes. Our showroom in Mason has a full simulator bay you can hit balls in. Book a visit, bring your clubs, and see how it feels before you decide.

Do you handle the whole project?

Yes. We design the space, supply the equipment, install the screen, enclosure, projector, turf and launch monitor, set up the software, and show you how to use it. After that, we're a phone call away for support and upgrades.
