Questions

Before you begin

What is a golf entertainment space?

A room built around golf and the people who gather to play: the simulator bay, lighting, sound, seating, a place to set a drink, and finishes that make it feel like part of your home or business. We design and build the whole room, not just the simulator.

Why “Emergency Nine”?

In golf, an emergency nine is the quick round you squeeze in when there’s no time for eighteen. A golf room at home or at work makes that round possible any evening of the week.

How much space do I need?

A common starting point is a ceiling of about 9–10 feet, a width of 12–16 feet and a depth of 15–20 feet. Ceiling height matters most for a full driver swing. We measure your space and tell you honestly what will work before you commit to anything.

Which simulator do you use?

Most of our builds use the ProTee VX, an overhead launch monitor mounted on the ceiling above the hitting area. Nothing sits on the floor, so the room stays open and uncluttered.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the finishes and the technology. After a consultation and a look at your space, you receive a written proposal with clear options.

Can I see a golf room before I commit?

Yes. Our showroom in Mason is a working golf room. Book a visit, bring your clubs, and play a few holes.
