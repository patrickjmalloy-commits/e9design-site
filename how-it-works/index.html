How it works

Simple from the first call to the first swing.

You can be as involved as you like. Some clients want to choose every detail; others hand us the keys. Either way, you always know what’s next.

Call or book a visit

Tell us a little about your space and how you want to use it. We’ll set up a time for you to come to the showroom.

Try it in the showroom

Bring your clubs and hit balls on a full simulator. We’ll show you the difference between launch monitors and talk through your options.

Site visit & measurements

We come to your home or business, measure the room, check ceiling height, power and lighting, and note anything that needs work.

Design & written quote

You get a drawing of the finished space and a written quote with clear options at different budgets. Change as much as you like before you approve it.

Installation

One crew installs the screen, enclosure, projector, turf, launch monitor and computer, then tests everything before we leave.

Training & support

We walk you through the system, from playing a course to practising with swing data. After that, call us any time with questions or for upgrades.

See it before it’s built

A drawing before anything is ordered.

Before you approve a design, you see how the finished space will look: where the screen sits, where you stand to hit, and where everyone else sits to watch.

  • Layout for right- and left-handed players
  • Screen size, projector position and lighting
  • Seating, storage and finishes

See it, swing it, then decide.

Book a free visit to our Mason showroom. Bring your clubs and a photo or rough measurements of your space.
