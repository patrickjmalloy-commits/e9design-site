Process

From first call to first swing.

Be as involved as you like. Some clients choose every detail; others hand us the keys. Either way, you always know what comes next.

Consultation

Call us or book a consultation. Tell us about your space, how you want to use it, and your budget.

Showroom visit

Play in our showroom in Mason to see and feel a finished golf room, and talk through options in person.

Site visit & measurements

We visit your home or business, measure the space, and check ceiling height, power, lighting and anything that needs work.

Design & proposal

You receive a design of the finished room and a written proposal with clear options. Refine it as much as you like before you approve.

Build & install

We build the room and install the screen, enclosure, projector, turf and overhead simulator, then test everything.

Handover & support

We show you how to run everything, from a quick nine to practice with swing data. After that, we’re a phone call away.

Design first

See it before it’s built.

Before you approve a design, you see how the finished room will look: where the screen sits, where you stand to hit, and where everyone else gathers to watch.

  • Layout for right- and left-handed players
  • Screen size, projector position and lighting
  • Seating, storage and finishes

Let’s design your golf room.

Start with a consultation. Bring photos or rough measurements of your space, and we’ll show you what’s possible.
