Simulators

The right technology, not just the most expensive.

Every simulator is built from the same five parts. We help you choose each one for your room, your game and your budget, and make sure they all work together.

The heart of it

Overhead launch monitors, out of the way.

Most of our builds use the ProTee VX, an overhead launch monitor mounted on the ceiling above the hitting area. Nothing sits on the floor to trip over or hit, and the hitting area stays clean for right- and left-handed golfers.

What’s in a simulator

Five parts, one system.

Launch monitor

The heart of the system. We mainly install the ProTee VX, a ceiling-mounted camera system that measures ball speed, launch, spin and club path on every shot.

Impact screen & enclosure

A tough, quiet screen you hit real balls into, with side and ceiling netting or padding to keep the room safe.

Projector

A short-throw projector mounted out of the way, sized to the screen so the picture is bright and your shadow stays off it.

Hitting mat & turf

A mat that’s easy on your joints and gives honest feedback, set into turf that finishes the room.

Computer & software

Course play, practice ranges and swing analysis. We set it all up and show you how to use it.

Room finishing

Lighting, sound, seating, storage and wall finishes, so the space feels like a room and not a net in a corner.

Three ways to build

Which kind of simulator fits you?

Flex bay

For garages and shared rooms. A retractable screen and movable hitting area let the space go back to everyday use.

  • Garages and bonus rooms
  • Quick to set up and put away
  • Lowest starting cost

Dedicated room

A full simulator room in a basement or spare room, finished like the rest of your home.

  • Full enclosure and wall-to-wall turf
  • Lighting, seating and sound
  • Feels like part of the house

Commercial

For offices, bars, clubs and event spaces that want golf as a feature, built to handle heavy use.

  • Client entertainment and team events
  • Doubles as a meeting space
  • Durable, high-traffic materials
